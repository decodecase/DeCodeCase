# Witness Interview: Levent Ertuğrul

**Interviewee:** Levent Ertuğrul (Rival Radio Host, Metro FM)
**Interviewer:** Officer Çelik
**Date:** 14.03.2025
**Location:** Metro FM Studios

---
SPEAKER: Officer Çelik
Mr. Ertuğrul, you host "Şehrin Karanlığı" (City's Darkness) on Metro FM, a show often compared to Onur Aydın's "Gecenin Sesi."
---
SPEAKER: Levent Ertuğrul
(Leans back, looking smug) Compared, yes. Though I'd say mine has more journalistic integrity. Onur… he was all about the theatrics. Less substance, more smoke and mirrors. But yes, we were competitors. Healthy rivalry, mostly.
---
SPEAKER: Officer Çelik
Did you know Onur Aydın personally?
---
SPEAKER: Levent Ertuğrul
We'd met at industry events. Exchanged a few barbs, nothing serious. He was always a bit aloof, arrogant even. Thought he was a cut above everyone else because of his ratings.
---
SPEAKER: Officer Çelik
Were you aware of the project he was working on, known as RAKUN-11?
---
SPEAKER: Levent Ertuğrul
I'd heard whispers. Onur was being very cloak-and-dagger about something. Rumor was it was big, controversial. Something about old secrets being dug up. Frankly, I was curious to see if he'd finally overreach. He had a tendency to play fast and loose with facts if it made for a better story.
---
SPEAKER: Officer Çelik
Did you perceive him or his show as a professional threat?
---
SPEAKER: Levent Ertuğrul
A threat? (Chuckles) He had his audience, I have mine. His style was more… psychological melodrama. Mine is fact-based investigation. But sure, we were vying for the same late-night listener demographic. His numbers were good, I'll give him that. But sustainable? I doubted it. That level of intensity burns you out, or makes you enemies.
---
SPEAKER: Officer Çelik
Did you ever hear of any specific threats against Onur Aydın?
---
SPEAKER: Levent Ertuğrul
Only the general kind that comes with poking hornet nests. When you deal with the kind of stories Onur liked – corruption, hidden crimes, powerful people with secrets – you're bound to step on some dangerous toes. He seemed to relish that, though. The martyr complex.
---
SPEAKER: Officer Çelik
When did you hear about his death?
---
SPEAKER: Levent Ertuğrul
The morning after. Shocking, of course. Whatever our rivalry, I wouldn't wish that on anyone. It's a grim headline for the industry.
---
SPEAKER: Officer Çelik
Do you have any thoughts on who might have wanted to harm him, or why?
---
SPEAKER: Levent Ertuğrul
Whoever he was about to expose with this RAKUN thing, I'd wager. He was playing with fire, and it sounds like he got burned. Or maybe it was more personal. He wasn't exactly a warm, fuzzy guy. Could have been a disgruntled source, a jealous colleague… even a spurned lover, for all I know. His life was as dramatic as his shows.
---
SPEAKER: Officer Çelik
Were you, or anyone at Metro FM, actively trying to find out what RAKUN-11 was about?
---
SPEAKER: Levent Ertuğrul
(Shrugs) We have our sources. We keep an ear to the ground. If Onur was onto something truly explosive, we'd certainly be interested in the fallout, or perhaps a different angle. But actively trying to sabotage him or steal his story? Not our style. We have our own investigations to pursue.
---
SPEAKER: Officer Çelik
Thank you for your time, Mr. Ertuğrul.
---
SPEAKER: Levent Ertuğrul
Of course. It's a tragic story. I'm sure it'll make for a hell of a documentary someday.
