# Witness Interview: Haluk Sezgin

**Interviewee:** Haluk Sezgin (Legal Counsel, Radyo HERA)
**Interviewer:** Detective Demir
**Date:** 14.03.2025
**Location:** Police Headquarters

---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
Mr. Sezgin, we understand you met with Onur Aydın on March 12th, the day of his death.
---
SPEAKER: Haluk Sezgin
Yes, that’s correct. Onur requested a meeting. He wanted to discuss the legal parameters of a story he was developing – the one he referred to as “RAKUN-11.”
---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
What was the nature of your discussion? We’ve heard it described as “heated.”
---
SPEAKER: Haluk Sezgin
(Clears throat) “Heated” would be accurate. Onur presented me with a hypothetical scenario involving the public disclosure of sensitive personal information, potentially from sealed records. He was… insistent that the public interest outweighed privacy concerns. I strongly advised him against it. The station could face severe lawsuits, not to mention ethical breaches.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
What kind of information was he talking about?
---
SPEAKER: Haluk Sezgin
He was vague, but it seemed to involve psychiatric evaluations or patient histories. He believed this information was crucial to exposing some form of systemic abuse or cover-up. My position was, and remains, that such disclosures, if obtained illicitly or used irresponsibly, would be indefensible.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
Did he seem to accept your counsel?
---
SPEAKER: Haluk Sezgin
Not readily. Onur has always been… passionate about his work. He sees himself as a crusader. He argued that the “truth” of RAKUN-11 needed to be revealed, regardless of the legal niceties. I warned him that if he proceeded down the path he was suggesting, the station might not be able to support him, and he could face personal liability.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
Did he mention any specific individuals or organizations that might be implicated or might retaliate if this RAKUN-11 material was released?
---
SPEAKER: Haluk Sezgin
No. He kept the actual subjects of his investigation confidential, as he usually did. His concern was more about his right to publish, rather than direct threats. However, the level of secrecy and his almost fanatical conviction about this story… it was more pronounced than with his previous work.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
How did the meeting end?
---
SPEAKER: Haluk Sezgin
We were at an impasse. I told him I could not, as legal counsel, endorse the approach he was outlining. He said he would “take it under advisement.” He was clearly frustrated. I was also frustrated. He was an incredible talent, but his disregard for legal boundaries was a constant concern.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
Did you have any further contact with him after that meeting?
---
SPEAKER: Haluk Sezgin
No. That was the last time I saw him.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
In your professional opinion, was Onur Aydın about to commit a serious legal breach with his RAKUN-11 story?
---
SPEAKER: Haluk Sezgin
Based on what he described, yes. A very serious one. One that could have had devastating consequences for him and for Radyo HERA. I can only speculate, but perhaps someone he was investigating decided to silence him before he could unleash that story. It’s a grim thought, but in his line of work, a definite possibility.
