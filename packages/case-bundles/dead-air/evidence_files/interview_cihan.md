# Witness Interview: Cihan Karakaş

**Interviewee:** Cihan Karakaş (Producer, Radyo HERA)
**Interviewer:** Detective Demir
**Date:** 13.03.2025
**Location:** Police Headquarters

---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
Mr. Karakaş, thank you for coming in. Please tell us about your relationship with Onur Aydın and what you know about his recent work.
---
SPEAKER: Cihan Karakaş
Onur and I… we were a team. I’ve been his producer for five years. He was intense, brilliant, but also… troubled. Especially lately. He was working on something big, something he called the “RAKUN Project” or “RAKUN-11.” He was very secretive about it, more than usual.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
Did he share any details about RAKUN-11?
---
SPEAKER: Cihan Karakaş
Only fragments. He said it involved psychological profiles, old case files he’d somehow acquired. He was convinced it would be his most impactful work yet, but also the most dangerous. He mentioned being worried about protecting his sources, and himself. He even started talking about encryption, secure communications… not his usual style.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
When did you last see him?
---
SPEAKER: Cihan Karakaş
The day before… March 11th. We had a brief meeting. He seemed agitated, almost paranoid. He said he needed to “re-evaluate the narrative” for RAKUN and that he was considering pulling back on some of the more direct accusations he was planning to make. He mentioned a moral dilemma, about exposing someone’s trauma for a story.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
Did he mention any threats or anyone he was afraid of?
---
SPEAKER: Cihan Karakaş
Not directly. But his behavior… he was jumpier. He kept checking his phone, looking over his shoulder. He said something cryptic like, “Some stories don’t want to be told. Some people don’t want their masks removed.” I pressed him, but he just waved it off. Said he was just stressed.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
What about the broadcast on March 12th? Were you involved in its preparation?
---
SPEAKER: Cihan Karakaş
Not as much as usual. He was very hands-on with this one, said he needed to control every element. I knew the general theme was about the “echoes of the past” and how unresolved trauma can manifest, but he kept the specifics of the RAKUN connection very close to his chest. The technical setup for the studio that night… he insisted on overseeing it himself, which was unusual.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
Did you notice anything unusual with the equipment or studio access recently?
---
SPEAKER: Cihan Karakaş
Now that you mention it… there were a few odd things. The access logs for Studio 1 showed some late-night entries under Onur’s name, but at times I knew he wasn’t physically there. And a couple of times, he complained about his audio interface acting strangely, settings being reset. We chalked it up to glitches. Now… I don’t know.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
Do you know who might have wanted to harm Onur?
---
SPEAKER: Cihan Karakaş
Onur made enemies. His shows were provocative. He exposed people, institutions. The RAKUN thing… if it was as sensitive as he implied, then anyone connected to that, anyone who felt threatened by its exposure… they could be a suspect. He was diving deep into very dark waters.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
Anything else you think we should know?
---
SPEAKER: Cihan Karakaş
Just that… Onur was a good man, a brilliant journalist. He believed in the power of truth. Whatever this RAKUN project was, he thought it was a truth that needed to be told. I hope you find out who silenced him before he could.
