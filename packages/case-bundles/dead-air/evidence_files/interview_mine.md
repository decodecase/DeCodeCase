# Witness Interview: Mine Taşdemir

**Interviewee:** Mine Taşdemir (Head of Programming, Radyo HERA)
**Interviewer:** Detective Demir
**Date:** 14.03.2025
**Location:** Police Headquarters

---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
Ms. Taşdemir, thank you for your time. As Head of Programming, you had regular contact with Onur Aydın regarding his show, “Gecenin Sesi.”
---
SPEAKER: Mine Taşdemir
Yes, Onur was one of our star talents. “Gecenin Sesi” had a massive following. He was a unique voice, highly dedicated. Also, quite solitary in his methods. He preferred to develop his material independently.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
Were you aware of the “RAKUN-11” project he was working on?
---
SPEAKER: Mine Taşdemir
I knew he was working on a significant investigative piece. He was vague on the specifics, citing source protection and the sensitive nature of the material. He did mention it involved old institutional records and had the potential to be explosive. He requested a larger than usual budget for “research and security,” which I approved based on his track record.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
Did he express any concerns for his safety or mention any threats?
---
SPEAKER: Mine Taşdemir
About a month ago, he alluded to some “unwelcome attention” he was getting regarding his research. He didn’t elaborate. Onur was always a bit dramatic, so I didn’t press him too hard. However, last week, he specifically asked about the station’s security protocols, CCTV coverage around the studios, and access logs. That was unusual for him.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
Did he have any conflicts with other station personnel?
---
SPEAKER: Mine Taşdemir
Onur generally kept to himself. There was the usual creative tension with producers or sound engineers at times, but nothing beyond professional disagreements. I am aware he had a meeting with Haluk Sezgin, our legal counsel, on the day of the incident. Haluk later told me it was a heated discussion about the potential legal ramifications of Onur’s upcoming broadcast. Haluk felt Onur was being reckless.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
What was your opinion on the RAKUN-11 material, from what little you knew?
---
SPEAKER: Mine Taşdemir
Onur had a knack for finding stories that resonated deeply, even if they were disturbing. I trusted his journalistic instincts. However, I also knew he could be obsessive. The RAKUN project seemed to have taken a particularly strong hold on him. He spoke of it with a kind of feverish intensity.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
Do you know who might have access to Studio 1 or the technical knowledge to tamper with the equipment?
---
SPEAKER: Mine Taşdemir
Senior technical staff, including Beril Yaman, would have full access and knowledge. Onur himself was very tech-savvy and often managed his own studio setup. Beyond that, access is strictly controlled, especially for Studio 1 when it’s configured for a live broadcast like “Gecenin Sesi.” A full audit of keycard access logs for that day is underway.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Demir
In your view, was Onur Aydın a man who took unnecessary risks?
---
SPEAKER: Mine Taşdemir
He took calculated risks, in service of his stories. He believed in pushing boundaries. Whether this RAKUN story was a risk too far… I can’t say. He was passionate about it, believed it was something people needed to hear. The tragedy is that we may never know the full extent of what he uncovered.
