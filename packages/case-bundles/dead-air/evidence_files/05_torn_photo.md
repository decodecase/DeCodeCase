# Evidence Report #5 — Torn Family Photograph (Bedroom)

**Date Captured:** 12.03.2025
**Location:** Onur Aydın's apartment, bedroom – nightstand
**Medium:** Digital photograph of a partially torn physical photograph

## Visual Description:
A small framed photo was found on the nightstand beside Onur Aydın's bed. The photo, enclosed in a silver frame, depicts a family scene: two adults and two children. However, the image is visibly torn from the right side, with the face of one of the individuals deliberately ripped away. The torn piece is missing; it was not recovered from the room.

Upon examining the back of the torn family photograph, investigators discovered a handwritten phrase in faded pencil: "I AM BECOMING HIM." The handwriting appears deliberate and emotionally charged, suggesting a possible identity crisis or unresolved paternal conflict. The torn-out face on the photograph corresponds to the presumed father figure, further supporting the theory that the victim, Onur Aydın, may have been experiencing psychological distress related to his father.

## Notable Details:
*   Based on aging and appearance, the image likely dates back 20–25 years.
*   The individual whose face was removed appears to have been a male adult, possibly Onur's father.
*   The remaining figures show a younger Onur, a woman (likely his mother), and a small child (possibly a sibling).
*   The frame shows signs of wear and has smudges along the glass, indicating frequent handling.

## Relevance to Case:
*   This image may point to a deeply personal and unresolved familial conflict.
*   In Onur's journal entries and psychological notes, multiple references are made to paternal control, emotional suppression, and intergenerational fear.
*   The physical destruction of the father figure's image supports the emotional themes in his internal writings and provides material evidence of emotional instability.
*   While not directly connected to the crime, the symbolic damage represented by this object suggests possible motivations and psychological deterioration in the days leading to Onur's death.
