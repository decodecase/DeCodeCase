# Witness Interview: Murat Kaya

**Interviewee:** Murat Kaya (Station Manager, Radyo HERA)
**Interviewer:** Detective Kaya
**Date:** 13.03.2025
**Location:** Radyo HERA, Manager's Office

---
SPEAKER: Detective Kaya
Mr. Kaya, you were the first to find Mr. Aydın. Please walk us through what happened.
---
SPEAKER: Murat Kaya
(Visibly shaken) I… I still can't believe it. Beril, our sound engineer, radioed me. Said they'd lost Onur's feed from Studio 1. Dead silence. I went to check. The door was locked. Onur always locked it during broadcasts, but he should have responded to the intercom. I used the emergency key override. The room was dark… just the glow from some of his equipment. And he was… slumped over the console. Not moving.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Kaya
Did you touch anything?
---
SPEAKER: Murat Kaya
I checked for a pulse. Nothing. I immediately called emergency services. Then I sealed the room and told everyone to stay away.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Kaya
You mentioned Onur locked the door. Was that standard procedure for him?
---
SPEAKER: Murat Kaya
Yes, for "Gecenin Sesi." He said it helped him maintain the atmosphere, prevent interruptions. No one questioned it. He was our biggest star.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Kaya
Were you aware of any specific threats against Mr. Aydın or concerns he had about his safety?
---
SPEAKER: Murat Kaya
Onur lived in a world of dark stories. He often spoke metaphorically about "shadows" and "unseen forces." Recently, though, he seemed more genuinely anxious. He asked me to review our insurance policy regarding on-site incidents and journalistic protection. He was also very interested in the RAKUN-11 project he was developing, which he said was highly sensitive and could have "significant repercussions."
---
SPEAKER: Detective Kaya
Did he mention who or what these repercussions might involve?
---
SPEAKER: Murat Kaya
No specifics. Just that it was "old, dirty laundry" involving people who wouldn't want it aired. He was uncharacteristically secretive, even for him.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Kaya
We understand you were in the station's café earlier that evening and were seen on surveillance footage around the time Selin Aydın was also there.
---
SPEAKER: Murat Kaya
Yes, I often grab a coffee there. I saw Selin. She looked upset. We just exchanged a nod. She left shortly after. I didn't speak with her. I saw Onur at his usual table earlier, on his phone.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Kaya
Did Mr. Aydın have any known disputes with staff, or had he reported any unusual technical problems with Studio 1 recently?
---
SPEAKER: Murat Kaya
No major disputes. Onur could be demanding, but he was respected. As for technical issues, Beril mentioned he'd complained a few times about his audio interface acting up, but we thought it was minor. He did ask for a full diagnostic on the Studio 1 electrical systems about a week ago, which was unusual. He said he wanted to ensure "total signal integrity" for an upcoming broadcast.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Kaya
Who else had access to the emergency key for Studio 1?
---
SPEAKER: Murat Kaya
Myself, and the head of security. It's kept in a lockbox.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Kaya
Mr. Kaya, in your opinion, what happened in that studio?
---
SPEAKER: Murat Kaya
(Sighs heavily) Onur was a master of creating tension, of exploring the darkness. It seems the darkness finally reached out and touched him. This RAKUN project… it felt different. He was warier, more on edge than I've ever seen him. I think he knew he was playing with fire.
