# 🗂 Crime Scene Report

**Case Reference:** HERA/0312/2025
**Filed by:** Forensic Investigation Unit
**Location:** Radyo HERA, Broadcast Studio 1
**Date of Incident:** 12 March 2025
**Victim:** Onur Aydın

## SUMMARY:
At approximately 19:57 on 12 March 2025, a fatal incident occurred during a live radio broadcast at Radyo HERA. The victim, Mr Onur Aydın (37), was found unresponsive inside Studio 1 shortly after the live stream was interrupted. Emergency response was delayed due to locked access and unclear internal communication.

First responders noted signs of electrical malfunction near the victim's workstation. Preliminary findings suggested a sudden spike in voltage through the studio's audio interface system.

The broadcast had begun at 19:45. Witness accounts and surveillance footage confirm that Onur was alone in the studio at the time of death. However, digital system logs retrieved later suggest unauthorised remote access and irregular behaviour in audio input systems.

Cause of death remains under forensic review.
