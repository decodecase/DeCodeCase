# Witness Interview: Süheyla Bora

**Interviewee:** Süheyla Bora (Intern, Radyo HERA)
**Interviewer:** Officer Efe
**Date:** 13.03.2025
**Location:** Radyo HERA, Break Room

---
SPEAKER: Officer Efe
Ms. Bora, you're an intern here at Radyo HERA. Did you work with Onur Aydın?
---
SPEAKER: Süheyla Bora
Yes, a little. I mostly helped with research, fetching coffee, the usual intern stuff. Mr. Aydın was… well, everyone knows he was a genius. A bit intimidating, though. He was always very focused, very serious about his show, "Gecenin Sesi."
---
SPEAKER: Officer Efe
Were you here on the evening of March 12th?
---
SPEAKER: Süheyla Bora
Yes, I was. I was in the archive room, logging old tapes. That's on the other side of the floor from Studio 1. I didn't hear anything directly, but I remember when the power flickered for a moment. Then, a bit later, I heard people shouting. That's when I realized something was wrong.
---
SPEAKER: Officer Efe
Did you interact with Mr. Aydın that day?
---
SPEAKER: Süheyla Bora
Earlier, yes. Around 4 PM, I think. He asked me to find some very specific sound effects for him – things like old radio static, distorted whispers, the sound of a lock clicking. He was very particular about the "texture" of the sounds. He seemed stressed, more than usual. He kept rubbing his temples.
---
SPEAKER: Officer Efe
Did he say what these sounds were for?
---
SPEAKER: Süheyla Bora
Not really. Just that it was for a "special segment" and it needed to be "unsettling." He also asked me if I knew anything about old psychiatric hospital protocols or patient file classifications from the 90s. I didn't, of course. It was a strange question.
---
SPEAKER: Officer Efe
Did you notice anyone unfamiliar in the station that day, or anyone acting suspiciously?
---
SPEAKER: Süheyla Bora
Not really unfamiliar… but I did see Mr. Haluk Sezgin, the station's lawyer, go into Mr. Aydın's office around lunchtime. They were in there for a long time, and when Mr. Sezgin came out, he looked… angry. Flushed. Mr. Aydın stayed in his office and didn't come out for hours after that. I heard raised voices at one point, but couldn't make out what they were saying.
---
SPEAKER: Officer Efe
Raised voices between Mr. Aydın and Mr. Sezgin?
---
SPEAKER: Süheyla Bora
Yes. It wasn't a full-blown shouting match, but it was definitely tense. I remember thinking it was unusual because Mr. Aydın was usually very quiet and reserved, even when he was displeased.
---
SPEAKER: Officer Efe
Anything else you recall from that day, or any recent interactions with Mr. Aydın that seemed out of the ordinary?
---
SPEAKER: Süheyla Bora
He was very protective of a particular set of notes he carried in a worn leather briefcase. He usually left it on his desk, but for the past week or so, he kept it with him constantly. He once snapped at me when I accidentally bumped into it. He apologized immediately, which was also unlike him, but it showed how important that briefcase was to him. I assume it was related to his RAKUN project.
---
SPEAKER: Officer Efe
Thank you, Ms. Bora. That's helpful.
