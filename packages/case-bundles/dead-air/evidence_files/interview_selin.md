# Witness Interview: Selin Aydın

**Interviewee:** Selin Aydın (Victim's Wife)
**Interviewer:** Detective Kaya
**Date:** 13.03.2025
**Location:** Aydın Residence

---
SPEAKER: Detective Kaya
Mrs. Aydın, thank you for speaking with us during this difficult time. We need to understand more about Onur's state of mind and any conflicts he might have been involved in.
---
SPEAKER: Selin Aydın
(Voice strained) Conflicts? Onur's whole life was a conflict. With himself, with the world... with me.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Kaya
Can you elaborate on that?
---
SPEAKER: Selin Aydın
He was obsessed with his work. It consumed him. Those stories he told... they weren't just stories to him. They were pieces of people's lives, and he... he didn't always see the line between telling a story and exploiting it. He used to say he was giving voice to the voiceless. Sometimes I think he just amplified their pain, and ours.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Kaya
Were you aware of the specific project he was working on, the one he called "RAKUN-11"?
---
SPEAKER: Selin Aydın
RAKUN... (Scoffs bitterly) Yes, I knew. It was another of his descents into darkness. He found some old files, patient records from some... institution. He believed they held the key to understanding a pattern of abuse, of control. He thought it was his masterpiece. I thought it was him losing himself again.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Kaya
Did he mention anyone specific connected to this RAKUN project? Anyone he was afraid of, or who might have threatened him?
---
SPEAKER: Selin Aydın
He didn't name names, not to me. But he was more agitated than I'd seen him in years. Paranoid. He started locking his office door, encrypting his files. He kept muttering about "them" watching, about needing to protect the "integrity of the source." He said the RAKUN files were "cursed."
---
SPEAKER: Detective Kaya
We found a handwritten note of yours, Mrs. Aydın. It seemed to express some... distress regarding Onur's use of personal material.
---
SPEAKER: Selin Aydın
(Sighs) That. Yes. It was... a moment of frustration. He had a way of taking our lives, our arguments, my feelings, and twisting them into material for his show. That note... I never gave it to him. It was just me, trying to find words for how much it hurt. He dissected everything, including our marriage, for his art.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Kaya
Were you at Radyo HERA on the day of the incident?
---
SPEAKER: Selin Aydın
I went there earlier in the day, around 5 PM. I needed to drop off some papers he'd forgotten. We had a brief, tense conversation. He was distracted, dismissive. I left. I saw Murat Kaya in the café as I was leaving.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Kaya
Did Onur have any enemies that you know of? Anyone who would want to physically harm him?
---
SPEAKER: Selin Aydın
His work was controversial. He challenged powerful people. But this... this feels different. More personal. The RAKUN thing... it felt like it was consuming him from the inside out. Maybe it finally did.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Kaya
Do you have any idea what the password to his RAKUN-11 folder might be?
---
SPEAKER: Selin Aydın
(Shakes head) No. He would never have told me. That was his world. Separate. Locked away. Like so much of him.
---
SPEAKER: Detective Kaya
Is there anything else, Mrs. Aydın? Anything at all that might help us?
---
SPEAKER: Selin Aydın
Just... find out who did this. He was a difficult man, my husband. But he didn't deserve to die like that. No one does. And this RAKUN secret... I have a feeling it died with him. Maybe that's for the best.
